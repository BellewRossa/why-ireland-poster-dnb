# Why Ireland Poster Project – Dun & Bradstreet

## Overview
This repository contains all supporting materials for the MIS41010 group poster project. The project examines why Ireland serves as a strategic EMEA hub, using Dun & Bradstreet as a case study.

## Contents

### Meeting Minutes
Located in the `/docs` folder. These document the progression of the project across multiple group meetings, including company selection, research development, interview preparation, and final poster direction.

### Interview
- **QA_Anne_Kearney.docx**  
Contains the questions and responses from Anne Kearney (SVP, Tech Operations, Dun & Bradstreet). This served as a primary input into the poster’s analytical direction.

### Poster Development
- **Poster_Template.pptx**  
- **Script_Final.docx**  

These files represent the design and content development of the final poster.

### Brief
- **Poster_Brief.docx**  
The original assignment brief outlining the requirements and expectations of the project.

## Approach
- Iterative group collaboration across multiple meetings  
- Use of primary interview data to inform analysis  
- Supporting secondary research on Ireland’s role in global operations and data infrastructure  

## Objective
To demonstrate how Ireland enables Dun & Bradstreet to operate as a strategic data and operations hub within the European and global AI ecosystem.
